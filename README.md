**Sales and Marketing Call Center Dashboard**
![Sales and Marketing Call Center](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/62821d71-c2ee-45e0-9b54-25985f6d526a)

An Excel binary workbook (.xlsb) that delivers an interactive dashboard for tracking and analyzing call center performance trends. It highlights peak and low call volumes, breaks down metrics by team and campaign, and helps inform staffing and campaign decisions.

**Preview**

**Key Features: **

Monthly Call Trend: 
Dynamic line chart showing call volumes month‑over‑month.

Peak & Trough Highlights: 
Automatic markers for the highest and lowest call counts.

Team & Campaign Analysis: 
Pivot‑table summaries by agent, region, or marketing channel.

Interactive Slicers: 
Easy filtering by date range, campaign, call type, or agent.

Macros: 
One‑click refresh of all pivots and automated data‑compression routines.

**Getting Started:**

Download the repository to your computer or work directly in the browser.

Place the dashboard file (Sales and Marketing Call Center.xlsb) in a local folder of your choice.

Open it in Excel Desktop (Windows or Mac) and enable macros when prompted.

Load your own call data into the hidden data sheet (table named “Table1”).

Use the “Refresh All” button on the Dashboard sheet or run the built‑in macro to update pivots and charts.

**How to Use:**

Filter & Drill‑Down
Click on slicers to narrow the view by time period, agent, or campaign.

**Refresh Data:**

Press the refresh button or run the RefreshAllPivots macro to pull in new data.

**Explore Details:**

Right‑click on charts or pivot tables to drill into underlying data.
